This is Glance, your Telegram, answered from your glasses. Built for the Even Realities G2, Glance turns your Telegram into a one card at a time digest. Sender, first line, and an urgency tag, readable at a glance. No phone in your hand, and no endless feed, just the messages that matter, in the order they arrived. Tap once, and Gemini drafts a short reply for you. The draft appears on its own card, so nothing is ever sent without your confirmation. One more tap, and the reply is delivered through the Glance Telegram bot, straight into the same chat. Glance also keeps an eye on the market. Live prices from HyperLiquid, read only, with no trading. Bitcoin. Ethereum. Solana. Hype. And XRP, each with its twenty four hour change, one card per coin. And when a watched coin moves sharply, a price alert flows into the same digest, so the market comes to you. One glance, one tap. That is Glance.
